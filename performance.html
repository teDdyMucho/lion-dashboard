How to read this page
Completion rate
Jobs finished ÷ jobs scheduled. 90% or more is the target; 80–89% is acceptable; under 80% needs attention.
Last 7 / 30 days
The same rate, but only counting the trailing 7 or 30 days. Compare it with the all-time rate to see if things are improving.
New vs. finished
New = work orders created that day. Finished = work orders closed that day. More finished than new means the backlog is going down.
Within estimate
Each job gets an estimated level (1 = about 1 hour, 2 = about 2 hours …). A job is "within estimate" when the actual clocked time landed in that hour band.
